1. Project Abstract & Objective

Criminal record systems generally contain personal information about suspects along with their photograph. To identify an unknown criminal, eyewitness testimony provides essential clues. Often, image recordings are degraded or unavailable. To solve this, the Criminal Face Identification System divides human facial mugshots into distinct slices (Hair, Eyes, Nose, Lips, and Chin). Eyewitnesses select feature clippings to compose the suspect's face. The system executes a frequency-based slice matching algorithm calculating the contributor frequency and predicts suspect identity with up to 99% confidence.
